A measurement I worked on this year became the first physics result to be announced during LHC Run 3, making it also the first result at the highest operating energy ever reached by a particle accelerator!

You can find summaries from multiple sources which explain why the measurement was so special:

- **[CERN News](https://home.cern/first-run-3-physics-result-cms/ )**
- **[CMS Physics Briefing (detailed with pictures!)](https://cms.cern/news/top-quarks-fast-arrive-new-energy-frontier )**
- **[DESY News (also in German)](https://particle-physics.desy.de/e221976/e221978/e319859/e320088/index_eng.html?preview=preview )**

This measurement was a major achievement for CMS and for DESY, where the top quark physics group led the effort. I also had the opportunity to help write the CMS and DESY articles.
