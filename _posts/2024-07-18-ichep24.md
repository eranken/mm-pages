## [Recent results on inclusive and differential top-quark pair production from 5 to 13.6 TeV at CMS ](https://indico.cern.ch/event/1291157/contributions/5878612/)

I recently gave a presentation at one of the largest particle physics conferences in the world! You can even take a look at my slides above if you want.

ICHEP, the International Conference on High Energy Physics, brings together physicists from different branches of particle physics once every two years. Held in Prague this year, I traveled there to present some recent results of the CMS experiment in top quark physics!
