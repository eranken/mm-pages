## [Episode: Evan and the Particle Physicists](https://science-chatter.blogs.uni-hamburg.de/?p=1764)

I recorded a podcast episode with Science Chatter Hamburg! Here I discuss a common misconception about the realities of particle physics research at the [LHC](https://home.cern/science/accelerators/large-hadron-collider/), inspired by a conversation with my Dad. 

Joined by science communicator [Michael Bueker](michael-bueker.de), I do my best to explain in plain concepts why particle physics data doesn't how many people expect it to. I am then joined by my good friends and collaborators Aliya Nigamova and Federico Vazzoler for a brief discussion of particle physics research realities at the LHC.

Check it out with the link above, via the Univerity of Hamburg!
